Support

Need help or have feedback?

Email us at support@example.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do I select a word?

Touch the first letter, drag to the last letter in a straight line, then lift your finger. Words can run in any of 8 directions, including backwards.

How do I unlock more levels?

Complete a level to unlock the next one. You can replay finished levels anytime from Select Level.

How are stars earned?

Finish within the target time without hints for 3 stars. Using one hint, or taking up to twice the target time, earns 2 stars. Any other completion earns 1 star.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Progress is stored only on your device. Use Settings → Export My Data to save a copy of your data.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete All Data. This permanently erases your name, progress and settings from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Word Seekers works completely offline.
