Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

🛡️
Word Seekers does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Word Seekers has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game works completely offline and never sends data over the internet, so we never receive your name or any other information about you.

What is stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Your name or nickname, if you choose to enter one. It is optional and used only to greet you in the game.
  • Game progress: unlocked and completed levels, stars, best scores, words found and Daily Challenge streaks.
  • Settings: sound, haptic feedback, theme, and which tutorials you have seen.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Your choices and control

  • Your name: you can skip it, change it or remove it at any time in Settings → Profile.
  • Export: Settings → Export My Data creates a file with everything the game stores, including your name, and opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete All Data permanently erases your name and all game data from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Children

Word Seekers is suitable for all ages. Entering a name is optional; children can use a nickname or skip it. Any name entered stays on the device and is never sent to us or anyone else, so no information about children is collected. A parent can remove it in Settings → Profile or with Delete All Data.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a game update and posted on this page with a new effective date.
